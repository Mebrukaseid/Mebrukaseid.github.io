Respiratory Tract Infections

Explore common infections of the upper and lower respiratory tract, including their symptoms, prevention, treatment, and medicine safety.

What are respiratory tract infections?

Respiratory tract infections are infections affecting parts of the respiratory system. They are commonly divided into infections of the upper respiratory tract and the lower respiratory tract.

Different respiratory infections have different causes, symptoms, severity, and treatment approaches. Some are caused by viruses, while others may be caused by bacteria or other microorganisms.

Medicine safety in respiratory infections

Not every respiratory infection requires an antibiotic. Many respiratory infections are caused by viruses, for which antibiotics are not effective.

Treatment should depend on the specific infection, severity, age, underlying conditions, and other medicines being used.

Important:

Avoid using antibiotics without appropriate medical assessment. Unnecessary antibiotic use can contribute to antimicrobial resistance and may cause adverse effects.

When to seek medical care

  • Difficulty breathing
  • Chest pain
  • Bluish lips or face
  • Confusion or unusual drowsiness
  • Severe weakness
  • Symptoms that are severe or rapidly worsening
