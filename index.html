MEDICATION SAFETY

When should you take your medicines?

The timing of a medicine can affect how well it works, how well it is tolerated, and how it fits into a daily routine.

Timing matters in medication use.

Some medicines are taken with food, while others work best on an empty stomach. Some are commonly scheduled in the morning or evening, while others need consistent intervals throughout the day.

Understanding these instructions can help support safer and more effective medicine use.
